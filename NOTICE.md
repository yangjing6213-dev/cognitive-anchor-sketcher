`Cognitive Anchor Sketcher` is a Codex Skill for cognitive-anchor-based article illustrations.

The runtime package is the `cognitive-anchor-sketcher/` directory and its supporting references. The optional files under `docs/examples/` are public showcase assets supplied and authorized by the project author; they are not runtime dependencies.

The distribution excludes private local material, historical articles, raw generated-image directories, browser records, external repositories, credentials, and local audit reports. Custom IP references remain project-local unless their owner explicitly authorizes redistribution.
